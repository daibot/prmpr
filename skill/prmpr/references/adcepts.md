# Adcepts

An adcept is an ad concept shown fast: one key visual, one headline, sometimes a line of body copy, the tagline and the logo. Its job is to sell the **idea** in a review, not to be the final ad.

## What a route needs

For each route:

```
ROUTE <n>: <route name>
THE IDEA: <one sentence; would make sense told across a table>
INSIGHT IT USES: <which audience truth>
HEADLINE: <primary>
  alts: <2 to 3 alternates>
BODY (optional): <one or two sentences>
TAGLINE / ENDLINE: <if the campaign has one>
KEY VISUAL: <what we see, described for a designer or art buyer>
LAYOUT: <where the headline, visual, logo and CTA sit; type scale and hierarchy>
CTA: <if the format calls for one>
WHY IT DRIVES THE OUTCOME: <one or two lines tied to the success criteria>
CAMPAIGNABILITY: 2 to 3 more executions in one line each (new headline + visual) to prove it is a platform, not a one-off
FORMATS: how it adapts (OOH 48-sheet, print page, social 1:1 / 4:5 / 9:16, display)
PROMPTS: per-model prompts for the key visual (see model-prompts.md)
```

## Spread of routes

Show three routes that differ in **strategy**, not just in look:
1. **On brief**: the clearest expression of the SMP.
2. **Stretch**: a fresher angle on the same insight.
3. **Bold**: the one that scares the client a little. Say what makes it risky.

## Craft checks (add to the success criteria)

- **1 + 1 = 3**: the headline and visual do not say the same thing. Together they make a point neither makes alone.
- **Two-second read**: the main point lands in two seconds. For OOH, headline 7 words or fewer.
- **Brand linkage**: you could not swap in a competitor's logo without the ad breaking.
- **One idea per ad**: no stacked messages.
- **Mandatories fit**: there is space for logo, legal and CTA without crushing the idea.
- **Truthful claims**: no invented stats; claims are marked "needs substantiation".
- **Not a cliché**: list the category's standard images (for example a smiling family at the table) and avoid them unless the route deliberately subverts them.

## Board layout (for Figma or a document)

Per route: a title card (route name + idea in one line), then one frame per execution at the hero format, then a strip of format adaptations. Headline and copy set as live text over an image placeholder or the generated visual. A notes area under each frame holds the rationale and the prompt.

Default hero format: 4:5 social unless the brief says otherwise. OOH is 3:1 or 2:1 landscape, print is the page size.

## Visual generation notes

- Generate the key visual **without text**. Set the type in layout.
- Lock the recipe and change only subject, action and composition between executions so the campaign looks like one family.
- Leave negative space where the headline goes, and say so in the prompt ("clear negative space top third for headline").
